One For All 🏆 1st Place – OSTİMTECH Jam 2025

OSTİMTECH Jam 2025 48 hours 12 entries 3-person Team Windows / Mobile Pixel Art

Won 1st place out of 12 entries at OSTİMTECH Jam 2025, a 48-hour game jam, with a 3-person team.

Concept (theme: "One Shot"): you play as clones, each holding a gun with a single bullet and nothing to shoot but yourself. Every clone you sacrifice leaves a body behind, and bodies are the tools you use to solve the puzzles.

  • Onboarding: the first screen shows a platform you can't reach by jumping, with a few bodies already lying below it. A hint teaches you to shoot yourself, and you learn to stack bodies to climb up.
  • Progression: 5 levels, each introducing one new mechanic that builds on the core idea: teach it, then combine it with what came before.
  • My level: designed a button level. Pressure plates only open the door while a body is lying on them; a toggle button teaches players not to waste a clone.
  • Iteration: puzzles became too limited, so we added the ability to move bodies, which opened up more solutions.
  • My role: came up with the core idea and developed it with the team; created all pixel art and the menu/UI; designed one level and coded the new mechanic for each level.
  • Result: the jury highlighted the mechanic and how well it fit the theme.
